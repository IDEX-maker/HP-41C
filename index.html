Printer (HP 82143A)

Everything the calculator prints appears on the paper below. The printer ROM is built in; PRX, PRA, PRSTK, ADV and the other print functions run the real printer firmware. Printing to paper or a PDF uses your browser's print dialog, so on a PC you can pick any printer, and on Android any Wi-Fi printer that Android can see.

ROMs & setup

The 41CX system ROMs (NUT0-N, NUT1-F, NUT2-L, XFNS3-3B, TIME-3B, XFNS5-2D) and the HP 82143A printer ROM (PRINTER) are built in. Other ROM modules can be added from your own .ROM dumps (8192 bytes each) or .MOD files; those files are read locally in your browser and remembered on this device only.

ROM notice: HP-41 ROM images are most likely still protected by copyright (by HP and by the authors of third-party modules). The built-in ROMs come from the set published at systemyde.com, which states no licence. The maintainer of this project treats them as public domain, but that is unverified. Check the rules where you live and use them at your own risk.
